## Dr Seyedmostafa Safavi

Assistant Professor, School of Technology, Asia Pacific University of Technology and Innovation (APU), Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

I work on the security of AI systems and on AI for security: indirect prompt injection in LLM agents, explainable AI for cybersecurity, privacy engineering for mobile and wearable devices, federated learning and blockchain for healthcare.
